How It Works

Answer three quick questions and our rule-based engine will match you with the best restaurants on the platform.

Dietary preference filters out unsuitable venues. Selecting Vegan or Halal strictly limits results to compliant restaurants.

Budget range compares your expected spend per person against each restaurant's average price range.

Dining purpose matches the venue's atmosphere and style to your occasion.

Up to three recommendations are shown, ranked by relevance. Select one and click Reserve This Restaurant to pre-fill the booking form.
